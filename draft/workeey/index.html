This is Workeey

I'm Yossi Grunwald, the founder of Workeey, an app that runs a small trade business in one place. I started it in June 2026, and a few months later it was a live product. It is free to start, and it follows a job from quote to payment.

Who it is for

Workeey is made for plumbers, electricians, HVAC techs, handymen and freelancers: people who work for themselves. The person who does the job is also the one who quotes it, schedules it and collects the payment.

Why I built it

People in the trades should be able to finish the job and go home, not start a second shift of paperwork. That is why I built Workeey.

That second shift is quotes, invoices and chasing payment. The work happens on site, so the paperwork should be finished there too.

I built Workeey as one app for the phone. Every design decision in it is measured against one rule: fewer taps, less clutter, less mental load.

What it does today

A job starts with a price. Workeey builds an estimate from the tradesperson's own rates right on the job, ready to send as a quote PDF. A separate free tool from Workeey, Quote Links, turns a quote into a link the customer can approve online, with no app and no account.

Then the job goes on the calendar. When the work is done, the invoice goes out with a way to pay online, and reminder texts can follow if it is not paid.

Workeey also keeps client records, photos, notes, receipts and expenses, and on its paid plans it links to QuickBooks and Google Calendar. Anything added with no signal is saved and syncs when the phone is back in range.

An assistant on every screen takes a request in plain words and prepares the job, the client or the invoice. One tap confirms it.

Where it is now

Workeey is live on the web, and the Android app is on Google Play. As I write this, in November 2026, the iPhone app is in review at Apple and the public site is rolling out in 13 languages.

I run Workeey from Brooklyn, New York. Every product decision is mine, and I am still building it. If you want to hear more, tell me a good time to talk.

Yossi Grunwald, founder of Workeey
