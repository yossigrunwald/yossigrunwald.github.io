Workeey: the simple app for tradespeople

I'm Yossi Grunwald, the founder of Workeey, a simple all-in-one app for self-employed tradespeople. It runs a small trade business from the phone: the schedule, clients, estimates, invoices and getting paid. It is free to start, and it follows a job from quote to payment.

Who it is for

Workeey is made for plumbers, electricians, HVAC techs, handymen, landscapers, cleaners and other trades in the US: people who work for themselves. The person who does the job is also the one who quotes it, schedules it and collects the payment, so Workeey keeps all of that in one place.

Workeey's own site shows how it fits each trade, with pages on invoicing for plumbers, electricians, HVAC techs and handymen, and on how it fits other trades.

What Workeey does

  • Estimates and quote PDFs: an estimate built from the tradesperson's own rates, right on the job, ready to send as a quote PDF.
  • Quote Links: a separate free tool from Workeey that turns a quote into a link the customer can approve online, with no app and no account.
  • Job scheduling: the day's jobs in time slots, and on the paid plans a sync with Google Calendar.
  • Invoicing: a finished job becomes an invoice in one tap, emailed with a way to pay online by card or bank.
  • Payment reminders: when an invoice is not paid, reminder texts can follow.
  • Client records: each client's contact details, address, notes and photos in one place, with their jobs, invoices and payments a tap away.
  • Job photos: before-and-after photos taken in the app, backed up off the phone and added to invoices as proof of work.
  • Integrations: a bank connection, so payments turn up on their own and expenses are easy to see, and on the paid plans QuickBooks and Google Calendar.
  • Works with no signal: anything added with no signal is saved and syncs when the phone is back in range.
  • An assistant on every screen: it takes a request in plain words and prepares the job, the client, the invoice or the quote. One tap confirms it.

There is more on every Workeey feature, and on how a day with the app goes.

How I built it

People in the trades should be able to finish the job and go home, not start a second shift of paperwork. That is why I built Workeey.

That second shift is quotes, invoices and chasing payment. I built Workeey myself, from first idea to live product, starting in June 2026. Every product decision is mine.

The work happens on site, so the paperwork should be finished there too. That is why Workeey is one app for the phone, and why every design decision in it is measured against one rule: fewer taps, less clutter, less mental load.

Behind that simple screen are the hard parts: online card and bank payments, reminder texts, a bank connection, QuickBooks and Google Calendar sync, photo backup, and work saved with no signal that syncs later.

Where it is now

Workeey is live on the web and on the Google Play Store. Coming soon to iPhone.

Questions about Workeey

What is Workeey?

Workeey is a simple all-in-one app for self-employed tradespeople in the US. It runs the business from the phone: the schedule, clients, estimates, invoices and getting paid.

Who is Workeey for?

Plumbers, electricians, HVAC techs, handymen, landscapers, cleaners and other trades: people who work for themselves and do their own quoting, scheduling and billing.

Is Workeey free?

It is free to start. The free plan is not a trial: jobs, clients, the schedule, notes and invoicing stay free. Paid plans add more, such as QuickBooks and Google Calendar sync.

Does Workeey work on iPhone?

The iPhone app is coming soon. Until then, Workeey runs in the iPhone's browser. The Android app is on the Google Play Store.

Can a customer approve a quote online?

Yes, with Quote Links, a separate free tool from Workeey. The customer opens the link on any phone or computer and approves, declines or asks for changes, with no app and no account.

Does Workeey work without signal?

It needs signal to open. Anything added with no signal is saved and syncs when the phone is back in range.

Who made Workeey?

Workeey was founded by Yossi Grunwald in Brooklyn, New York. He started it in June 2026, built it from first idea to live product, and makes every product decision.
