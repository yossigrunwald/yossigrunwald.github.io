Workeey

An app that runs a small trade business from one place. Built by one person.

“People in the trades should be able to finish the job and go home — not start a second shift of paperwork.”

Workeey is free for plumbers, electricians, HVAC techs, handymen and freelancers. Jobs, clients, quotes, invoices and getting paid by card, in one place. It's live on the web and on Google Play.

I built all of it myself: the app, the website, the Android app, and the ads.
